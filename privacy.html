Privacy

Last updated October 9, 2026

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we store the email address you type, which button you came from (for example the free or team plan), and the time you signed up. This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics or ad scripts.

How we use it

Only to email you about DunGenAI early access and launch. We don’t sell, rent or share your email with anyone for their own marketing.

Where it lives

The waitlist is stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when your browser requests them.

Removing your email

Reply to any email from us and ask to be removed, and we’ll delete your address from the list.

Changes

If this changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
