# Texture credits

## detail/stone.png

Fine surface detail for stone (painted-detail.ts), made from **Stylized Stone Wall 001** by Gendo
(3dtextures.me), https://3dtextures.me/2021/01/12/stylized-stone-wall-001/ — CC0
(https://3dtextures.me/about/: "All textures on this site are licensed as CC0").

Changed: reduced to 512 px greyscale and high-passed (each pixel minus its blurred neighbourhood,
wrapped so it still tiles), with the dark side softened, so only the painted chisel and grit detail
remains and the block layout fades; the KayKit models carry their own blocks.
